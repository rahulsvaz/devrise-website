Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy applies to all services purchased from Devrise, operated by ZY Technolabs.

1. How to Request a Refund or Cancellation

To request a refund or cancel an order, email us at mimo.admin@gmail.com with your name, registered phone number, payment ID or order details, and the reason for your request. You can also call us at +91 88807 72200 for help.

2. Cancellation

You can cancel an order by email before work on your project has started for a full refund. If work has already started, we may deduct a fair amount for the work completed so far.

3. Eligible Refunds

Refunds are issued if we are unable to deliver the service, if a payment was charged twice or by mistake, or if the order is cancelled as described above. Monthly maintenance plans can be cancelled at any time and will not renew for the next month; fees for the current month are not refunded.

4. Refund Timeline

Once your refund is approved, it is processed within 5 to 7 working days to the original payment method (card, UPI, bank account or wallet). The time for the amount to appear in your account may depend on your bank.
