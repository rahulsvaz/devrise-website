Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Devrise, operated by ZY Technolabs, provides digital services only. We do not ship any physical products.

1. Delivery Method

All services are delivered online: by email, through a shared link, by deploying the website to your hosting or domain, or by handing over source files.

2. Delivery Timelines

Work begins after payment is confirmed and the project scope is agreed by email. Typical delivery times are 7 working days for a Starter Website, 15 working days for a Business Website and 21 working days for an E-commerce Store. Maintenance services start within 2 working days of payment.

3. Delays

Timelines may be extended if required content or feedback is delayed. We will tell you by email if any delay is expected.
